Technical diagrams and scenarios should teach a transferable skill. Avoid drifting into endless subnet/VLAN variants, product minutiae, acronym recall or unsupported incident summaries. Revisit useful lessons after spacing, while tracking undercovered objectives. A recent article is an idea source; its factual passages still need to support the actual question.

Publication dates inform topic selection. A document being fetched today does not make it recent news. Industry cases can be untagged when there is no genuine exam alignment; they retain the same factual, answer-uniqueness, diagram and blind-solver checks. Do not attach exam claims or certification hashtags merely to increase reach.

Cisco [announced CCNA v2.0 for February 3, 2027](https://blogs.cisco.com/learning/stay-on-track-get-certified-before-the-ccna-refresh), with v1.1 testing ending February 2. The announcement emphasizes more practical troubleshooting, security and AI. Treat this as an upcoming transition, not an already active syllabus. The configured v1.1 PDF remains the current baseline in this release. Weekly fetching detects changes at configured blueprint URLs; discovery and adoption of replacement exam-version URLs are not yet automatic.

CompTIA scope currently means Network+ and Security+. Adjacent endpoint topics are welcome when relevant to this account; do not claim comprehensive coverage of every CompTIA certification. Use public outlines and original educational cases, never exam dumps.
